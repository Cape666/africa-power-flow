Use for normal body text.

The overall color balance should be approximately:

70% white/light grey
25% navy/charcoal
5% orange

Do NOT use blue and green as the primary brand combination. The company is an industrial electrical and mechanical engineering business, not an environmental or renewable-energy-only company.

Orange should be an accent, not the dominant color.

The overall appearance should communicate:

Engineering • Industrial • Professional • Reliable • Technical • Modern • African

DESIGN

Make the website:

Simple

Mobile-friendly

Easy to navigate

Do not overuse animations.

Do not use excessive gradients or unnecessary decorative elements.

Do not make unsupported claims about facilities, offices, employees or operations in countries where the company does not actually have them.

IMPORTANT

Keep Version 1 simple.

Do not build supplier databases, AI matching, customer accounts, dashboards, marketplaces or complex automation yet.

The primary purpose of this website is:

Build credibility → attract industrial customers → generate enquiries → attract manufacturers → develop sales opportunities across Africa.
